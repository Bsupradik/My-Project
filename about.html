Visit France

Visiting France, the world's top tourist destination, offers iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and Louvre, diverse regions from Provence lavender fields to Riviera beaches, and world-class wine country. Spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) offer pleasant weather. Travel planning should include booking popular sites in advance.

Essential Travel Information

  1. Best Time to Visit: Spring for blooming gardens, summer for coastal activities, or autumn for wine harvest.
  2. Entry Requirements: Ensure passport validity for at least 3 months beyond departure. Be aware of the new Entry/Exit System (EES) for the Schengen area starting late 2025.
  3. Top Destinations: Paris (landmarks, museums), Provence (landscapes, villages), French Riviera (beaches), Bordeaux (wine)
  4. Getting Around: High-speed trains (TGV) are excellent for city-to-city travel.

Key Tips and Recommendations

  1. Attractions: Book tickets for major attractions, including the Eiffel Tower and Louvre, well in advance to skip long lines.
  2. Safety: Be cautious of petty theft in tourist areas and on public transport.
  3. Dining: Popular restaurants often require reservations.

Recommended Itineraries

  1. 1 Week: Focus on Paris, Normandy, and the Loire Valley.
  2. 10-14 Days: Combine Paris, Provence, and the French Riviera.

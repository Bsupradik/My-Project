Services

Visitors to France from Nepal must apply for a visa through VFS Global in Kathmandu, acting for the French Embassy in New Delhi. Appointments are mandatory, ideally scheduled 15 days before travel via the France-Visas portal. A Schengen visa is typically required for tourism, family visits, or business.

Key Services & Procedures

  • Visa Application Portal: France-Visas is the official site to fill out forms and create an account.
  • VFS Global Kathmandu: Accepts applications, collects fees, and gathers biometric data (fingerprints) on Tuesdays and Fridays (9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.).
  • Requirements: A passport valid for at least 3 months beyond departure, travel insurance, proof of accommodation, and proof of funds are necessary.
  • Processing Time: Generally 15 days, though it may take longer.
  • Visa at Your Doorstep: VFS offers an optional, paid service for submitting applications from home or office.

Travel Planning

  • Tourism Information: France.fr provides comprehensive travel information, including destinations, climate, and transport.
  • Assistance: For personalized trip planning from Nepal, agencies such as President Travel & Tours provide services.
